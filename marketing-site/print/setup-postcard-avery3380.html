METARBoardAviation Weather & Traffic Display

Let's get your display on the air.

1

Plug it in.

Power, then HDMI to your TV or monitor. Give it about 30 seconds to boot.

2

Connect your phone to its Wi‑Fi.

Look for a network called METARBoard Setup — no password needed.

3

Open a browser to the setup page.

Go to 10.42.0.1 and the wizard will load.

4

Pick your Wi‑Fi, airport, and password.

Choose your home network, enter your airport code (e.g. KJFK), optionally name the display, and set an admin password.

AFTER SETUP

Keep this card — you may need it again.

🔒 Change settings later

When setup finishes, the wizard shows an address like metarboard‑frontdesk.local/admin — note it down.

From any device on the same Wi‑Fi network as the display, open a browser to that address and sign in with your admin password.

❓ Trouble connecting?

  • Screen stuck on setup? Reconnect to METARBoard Setup Wi‑Fi and finish the steps on the front.
  • Moved to a new router? The display drops back into setup mode automatically — just repeat the steps.
  • Lost power mid‑setup? No harm done — power it back on and pick up where you left off.
Need a hand?support@metarboard.io
